About CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a premier private university located in Changa, Gujarat. The university is approved by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is recognized as a Center of Excellence. With an educational legacy of more than 118 years, CHARUSAT is committed to providing quality education, research, innovation and student development.

Key Highlights

  • NAAC A+ Accredited University
  • 125-acre eco-friendly, Wi-Fi enabled campus
  • More than 8,000 active students
  • 72+ Undergraduate, Postgraduate and Ph.D. programs
  • 9 specialized institutes
  • Highest placement package: INR 23 LPA

Vision

To become a globally recognized university by providing quality education, promoting innovation, research and developing skilled professionals with strong ethical values.

Mission

  • Provide quality education through modern teaching methods.
  • Encourage research and innovation.
  • Develop leadership and technical skills.
  • Prepare students for global career opportunities.
  • Promote ethical values and social responsibility.
